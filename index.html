Orthopedic Care

Our orthopedic specialists provide advanced treatment for bone, joint, and muscle conditions — helping you recover faster and move pain-free.

Services List

  • Complex Fracture & Trauma Surgeries
    • Advanced treatment for road traffic accidents, high-impact injuries, and complicated fractures
    • Management of open fractures, multiple fractures, and failed previous surgeries
    • Modern fixation techniques using plates, nails, screws, and minimally invasive methods
  • Joint Replacement Surgeries (Knee & Hip)
    • Total and partial knee replacement for severe arthritis and joint damage
    • Hip replacement surgeries for fractures, avascular necrosis, and painful arthritis
    • Faster recovery protocols focused on early mobilization and pain relief
  • Spine Surgeries & Slip Disc Treatment
    • Treatment for slip disc, sciatica, spinal stenosis, and chronic back pain
    • Surgical and non-surgical management of cervical and lumbar spine disorders
    • Personalized rehabilitation programs for long-term spine health and recovery
  • Sports Injury & Arthroscopic Surgeries
    • Arthroscopic (keyhole) surgeries for ligament, meniscus, and shoulder injuries
    • Specialized care for ACL tears, sports trauma, and joint instability
    • Rehabilitation programs designed for faster return to sports and daily activities
  • Rehabilitation & Physiotherapy Guidance
    • Customized rehabilitation plans after fractures, surgeries, and joint problems
    • Exercises focused on improving strength, mobility, and functional recovery
    • Guidance for posture correction, gait training, and pain management
  • Congenital Bone & Joint Deformity Treatment
    • Evaluation and treatment of congenital deformities in children and adults
    • Management of bow legs, knock knees, clubfoot, and limb deformities
    • Long-term corrective planning to improve mobility and quality of life
  • Arthritis & Chronic Joint Pain Management
    • Comprehensive treatment for osteoarthritis, rheumatoid arthritis, and joint stiffness
    • Non-surgical options including medications, injections, and lifestyle guidance
    • Advanced surgical solutions when conservative treatment is not effective
  • Back Pain, Neck Pain & Sciatica Treatment
    • Diagnosis and treatment for chronic back pain, neck pain, and nerve compression
    • Management of sciatica, muscle spasms, and posture-related spine problems
    • Combination of medications, physiotherapy, and minimally invasive procedures
  • Pediatric Orthopedic Care
    • Specialized orthopedic care for children with bone, joint, and growth-related problems
    • Treatment of pediatric fractures, walking abnormalities, and developmental conditions
    • Child-friendly approach focused on safe recovery and healthy bone development
  • Osteoporosis & Geriatric Fracture Care
    • Diagnosis and treatment of osteoporosis to reduce fracture risk in elderly patients
    • Management of hip, wrist, and spine fractures in senior citizens
    • Comprehensive care focused on mobility, fall prevention, and healthy aging

Who We Are

Balaghat's Trusted Specialist Hospital

Nishok Hospital is a premier multi-specialty medical facility located in the heart of Balaghat, Madhya Pradesh. Established with a clear vision of bringing world-class medical care to the people of Central India, we have grown to become the most trusted name in Orthopedic and Dermatology healthcare across Balaghat, Gondia, Waraseoni, and Seoni. From complex fracture surgeries to advanced skin treatments, our doctors bring specialist expertise combined with deep compassion treating every patient with the care and dignity they deserve.

Our Mission

To provide accessible, expert, and compassionate medical care to every patient in Balaghat and surrounding regions ensuring no one has to travel far for specialist treatment.

Our Vision
