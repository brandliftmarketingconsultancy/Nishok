Personal Information

Dr. Shrey is a highly qualified and experienced Orthopedic Surgeon practicing at Nishok Hospital, Balaghat. With advanced training in orthopedic surgery and a deep commitment to patient-centered care, Dr. Shrey has established himself as one of the most trusted bone and joint specialists in the Balaghat district and surrounding regions of Madhya Pradesh.

Dr. Shrey pursued his medical education with a focus on orthopedic surgery, completing his MBBS and subsequent specialist qualifications with distinction. His clinical training covered a wide range of orthopedic conditions from acute traumatic fractures to complex joint replacement surgeries giving him the broad expertise needed to treat patients across all age groups.

Over the course of his career, Dr. Shrey has successfully treated hundreds of patients suffering from conditions ranging from simple fractures to severe degenerative joint disease. His patients appreciate not just his surgical skill, but his calm, reassuring manner and his ability to clearly explain diagnoses and treatment plans.

At Nishok Hospital Balaghat, Dr. Shrey sees patients for both surgical and non-surgical orthopedic conditions. He believes that surgery should always be the last resort, and where possible, uses conservative management approaches to help patients recover effectively. When surgery is necessary, he utilizes the latest techniques to ensure minimal recovery time and the best possible outcomes.

Dr. Shrey is known in Balaghat for his patient-first philosophy, his transparent communication, and his genuine concern for the wellbeing of everyone who walks through the doors of Nishok Hospital. Patients travel from Gondia, Waraseoni, Seoni, and across the Balaghat district to consult with him.
