Arthritis Treatment in Balaghat

Arthritis can severely limit movement and quality of life if not managed on time. At Nishok Hospital, Dr. Shrey diagnoses and treats osteoarthritis, rheumatoid arthritis, and other joint conditions with a personalized approach focused on pain relief and improved function.

Comprehensive Arthritis Management for Joint Health

At Nishok Hospital Balaghat, arthritis care includes medical treatment, targeted physiotherapy, and advanced pain relief options. Our goal is to reduce inflammation, protect joints, and restore active daily living under expert orthopedic supervision.

Symptoms

Symptoms of Arthritis

  • Joint pain, swelling, and stiffness
  • Reduced range of joint movement
  • Warmth and redness around joints
  • Fatigue and general weakness
Process

Treatment Approach

  • Medication management with anti-inflammatory and joint-protective drugs
  • Physiotherapy and targeted exercises
  • Joint injections for pain relief
  • Surgical intervention for severe cases
Benefits

Benefits of Arthritis Treatment at Nishok Hospital

  • Accurate diagnosis of arthritis type and severity
  • Personalized treatment to reduce pain and stiffness
  • Long-term joint care for better mobility
