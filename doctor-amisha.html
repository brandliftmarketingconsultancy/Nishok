Personal Information

Dr. Amisha is a highly trained and experienced Dermatologist and Skin Specialist practicing at Nishok Hospital, Balaghat. As the only dedicated skin specialist in the Balaghat region with advanced training in both medical and cosmetic dermatology, Dr. Amisha has transformed the skin and hair health of hundreds of patients across Balaghat, Gondia, and Waraseoni.

Dr. Amisha completed her MBBS followed by her MD in Dermatology, Venereology & Leprology (DVL), giving her comprehensive expertise across the full spectrum of skin, hair, and nail conditions. Her training included hands-on experience with the latest cosmetic dermatology procedures including laser treatments, PRP therapy, HydraFacial, and chemical peels.

What sets Dr. Amisha apart is her holistic approach to skincare. She doesn't just treat the symptom she investigates the root cause of the condition and creates personalized treatment plans for each patient. Whether a young patient struggling with acne or an older patient dealing with melasma or hair loss, Dr. Amisha takes time to understand each case and explain the treatment plan clearly.

Dr. Amisha is also deeply committed to making advanced cosmetic dermatology accessible in Balaghat treatments that were previously only available in big cities like Nagpur or Bhopal are now available right here at Nishok Hospital. Patients no longer need to travel far for HydraFacial, PRP hair treatment, or laser skin rejuvenation.

She is known among her patients as a warm, approachable doctor who makes them feel heard and confident about their treatment journey. She also conducts regular skin health awareness sessions, especially for women and young adults in the Balaghat community.

Treatments Offered by Dr. Amisha

Dr. Amisha specializes in diagnosis and treatment of:

  • Acne Treatment medical-grade acne management including topical, oral therapy & procedures
  • PRP Hair Treatment Platelet-Rich Plasma therapy for hair loss & thinning
  • HydraFacial advanced facial treatment for deep cleansing, hydration & glow
  • Melasma Treatment targeted treatment for facial pigmentation and dark patches
  • Laser Treatment laser skin resurfacing, spot removal & skin rejuvenation
  • Pigmentation Treatment treatment for uneven skin tone, dark spots & post-acne marks
  • Chemical Peels controlled exfoliation for brighter, younger-looking skin
  • Anti-Aging Treatments skin tightening, wrinkle reduction & skin rejuvenation
